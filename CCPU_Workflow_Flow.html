Reentry, Launch & Impact, and Classification stay dormant (locked/missile state).

STEP Passive Ranging — ~2 ms (worst case)

PROCESSGroup per-station observations of one target into a single central track — HOW:
1 · Collect latest validated tracklists of all STNs (0x02/0x03/0x04) for frame N-1 — every observation carries a PTP µs timestamp
2 · Convert each Az/El observation into an ECEF unit direction vector d (station position + mount attitude)
3 · Pairwise angular test: δ(i,j) = acos( dᵢ · dⱼ ) — inside gate (sensor accuracy + inter-station parallax) → same-target candidate
4 · Group observations into 3-station tracks; one observation maps to at most one central track (conflict resolution)
5 · Compute group centroid ECEF estimate, weighted by observation quality → central tracklist (≤20 entries)
